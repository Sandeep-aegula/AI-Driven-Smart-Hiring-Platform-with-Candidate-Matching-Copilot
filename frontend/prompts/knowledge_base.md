A: Go to Resume Parser page, select a target job, and upload PDF/DOCX/TXT files. Q: How does AI screening work? A: AI analyzes resumes against job requirements and assigns match scores. Q: Can I generate job descriptions? A: Yes, use the AI-powered JD generator on the Jobs page. Q: How do I schedule interviews? A: Navigate to Interviews page, select candidate and job, then schedule. Q: What file formats are supported? A: PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV for resumes. Q: Is my data secure? A: Yes, all data is stored locally in SQLite database. # BUSINESS RULES - Candidates can only be associated with one active job at a time - Interview feedback is required before offer generation - Employees must have a complete candidate record - Resumes are parsed within 30 seconds - Match scores range from 0-100 - Hiring velocity is calculated weekly
